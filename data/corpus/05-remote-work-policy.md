# Remote work policy

Owner: People team. Applies to office-based roles. Hub operations roles are on site by
nature and are not covered.

## Hybrid default

Office-based employees work from their contracted site at least **2 days per week**.
Teams choose their anchor days; most teams use Tuesday and Thursday. Managers may require
attendance for specific events with one week's notice.

## Fully remote arrangements

An employee may apply to be fully remote after **6 months** of employment. Approval
requires the line manager and the Chief People Officer. Fully remote employees visit
their contracted site at least **4 times per year**, with travel paid by the company
under the travel policy.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than the contract country is allowed for up to **20 working
days per calendar year** without a change of contract. Longer stays require People team
and Finance review because of tax and employment law. Employees must tell their manager
and record the dates in Atlas before travelling.

## Time zones

Remote employees are expected to overlap with core hours (10:00 to 15:00 Central
European Time) for at least **3 hours** each working day.

## Home office equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset. Employees may claim
up to **EUR 300 once every 3 years** for a desk or chair through the expense policy.
Internet costs are not reimbursed.

## Security at home

Remote employees must use the company VPN for internal systems, lock their screen when
away, and never let household members use the company laptop. Confidential documents
must not be printed at home. See the data classification policy.

## Ending an arrangement

Either the employee or the company can end a fully remote arrangement with **3 months'
notice**. The company will give a business reason in writing.
