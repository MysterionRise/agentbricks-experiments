# Hardware and devices

Owner: IT. Applies to all employees who are issued a company device.

## Standard issue

Every office-based employee receives a laptop on their first day. The standard models
are a 14-inch laptop for most roles and a 16-inch laptop for engineering and design
roles. Hub operations staff share site workstations and are issued a handheld scanner
instead.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced every **4 years**. A device can be replaced earlier if a repair
would cost more than **40 percent** of a new device. Employees keep their laptop when it
is replaced only if IT has wiped it and the Chief Technology Officer has signed off on
the device disposal batch.

## Requesting equipment

Requests go through the IT service desk in Atlas. Standard peripherals (monitor,
keyboard, mouse, headset) are approved automatically. Non-standard equipment needs line
manager approval and, above **EUR 800**, Chief Technology Officer approval.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk **within 24 hours** and, if
stolen, to the local police. IT will remotely wipe the device. A lost device is also a
security incident at severity level 3 and follows the incident response policy. The
employee is not charged for a lost device unless there was gross negligence.

## Personal devices

Personal phones may be enrolled in mobile device management to receive company email
and calendar. Personal laptops may not access internal systems. Company data must not be
stored on personal cloud accounts.

## Software

Only software from the approved catalogue in Atlas may be installed. Engineering roles
have local administrator rights; other roles request installs through the service desk.
Requests for new software are reviewed by IT and Security within **5 working days**.

## Returning devices

Devices are returned to IT on the last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid
return label and must ship the device within **10 days** of their last day.
