# Company overview

Halcyon Freight moves palletised goods between regional distribution centres for retail
and manufacturing customers. The company was founded in 2011 in Rotterdam and is
privately held.

## Sites

| Site | Function | Approximate headcount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotterdam (HQ) | Head office, engineering, finance, people team | 140 |
| Duisburg | Distribution hub, operations | 210 |
| Lyon | Distribution hub, operations | 95 |
| Gdansk | Engineering and customer support | 60 |

Total headcount at the start of 2026 was 505 people. Operations staff are the majority.

## Organisation

The executive team has five members: the Chief Executive, the Chief Operating Officer,
the Chief Financial Officer, the Chief Technology Officer, and the Chief People Officer.
Every employee has one line manager. Engineering reports to the Chief Technology Officer
and is organised into four teams: Platform, Routing, Customer Portal, and Data.

## Fiscal year and planning

The fiscal year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Budgets are set in October for the
following year. Headcount requests outside the budget cycle need Chief Financial Officer
approval.

## Core systems

- **Compass** is the internal routing and dispatch system. It is built and run by the
  Routing team.
- **Ledger** is the finance system. Finance owns it; engineering does not deploy to it.
- **Beacon** is the customer portal where customers book and track shipments.
- **Atlas** is the internal wiki and the canonical home of this handbook.

## Working hours

Office-based roles work 40 hours per week. Core hours, during which everyone is expected
to be reachable, are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time. Operations shifts at the hubs
follow the site's shift plan, not core hours.
