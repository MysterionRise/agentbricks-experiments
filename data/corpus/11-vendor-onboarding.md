# Vendor onboarding

Owner: Procurement. Applies to any new supplier of goods, services, or software.

## When this policy applies

A vendor must be onboarded before the first purchase order or contract is signed. One-off
purchases under **EUR 2,500** can be made on a corporate card without onboarding, as
long as no Confidential or Restricted data is shared with the vendor.

## Steps

1. **Request.** The sponsor opens a vendor request in Atlas with the business need, the
   expected annual spend, and the data the vendor will handle.
2. **Due diligence.** Procurement checks company registration, sanctions lists, and
   financial standing. This takes up to **5 working days**.
3. **Security review.** Required when the vendor will handle Confidential or Restricted
   data or connect to internal systems. The Security team reviews the vendor's controls
   and takes up to **15 working days**. Software vendors must provide a recent
   independent security audit report.
4. **Legal review.** Legal reviews the contract. A data processing agreement is
   mandatory when personal data is involved.
5. **Approval.** See thresholds below.
6. **Setup.** Finance creates the vendor in Ledger. The sponsor is the vendor owner and
   reviews the relationship annually.

## Approval thresholds

| Expected annual spend | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 25,000 | Department head |
| EUR 25,001 to EUR 100,000 | Department head and Chief Financial Officer |
| Above EUR 100,000 | Chief Financial Officer and Chief Executive |

Any vendor handling Restricted data needs Chief Technology Officer approval in addition
to the spend-based approver, regardless of amount.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are **30 days** from invoice. Shorter terms need Chief Financial
Officer approval. Prepayment is not allowed except for software subscriptions billed
annually in advance.

## Offboarding

When a vendor relationship ends, the sponsor confirms that the vendor has deleted or
returned Halcyon data, access to internal systems is revoked within **2 working days**,
and the vendor is marked inactive in Ledger.
