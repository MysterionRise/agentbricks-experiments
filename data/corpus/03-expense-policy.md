# Expense policy

Owner: Finance. Applies to all employees and to contractors whose contract allows
expenses.

## Principles

Expenses must be necessary for Halcyon business, reasonable in amount, and submitted
within **60 days** of being incurred. Late claims are rejected unless the Chief Financial
Officer grants an exception.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for any single expense of **EUR 25 or more**. Below
EUR 25 a receipt is still preferred but a short description is accepted. Credit card
statements are not receipts.

## Submission and approval

Expenses are submitted through the Ledger expenses module. The line manager approves
claims up to **EUR 1,000**. Claims above EUR 1,000 need a second approval from the
department head. Claims above **EUR 5,000** need Chief Financial Officer approval.

Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## What is reimbursable

- Business travel, following the travel policy.
- Meals with customers or candidates, up to **EUR 60 per person**.
- Professional memberships and certifications relevant to the role.
- Books and online courses up to **EUR 400 per year** per employee, no pre-approval
  needed below that amount.
- Mobile phone costs up to **EUR 40 per month** for roles with a phone allowance.
- Home office equipment, as described in the remote work policy.

## What is not reimbursable

- Alcohol, unless part of an approved customer event.
- Fines, including parking and speeding fines.
- Commuting between home and the employee's contracted site.
- Personal items, even if used partly for work.
- Lounge membership or airline status programmes.

## Corporate cards

Managers and frequent travellers may hold a corporate card. Card spend still requires a
receipt and goes through the same approval. Personal use of a corporate card is a
disciplinary matter.
