# Travel policy

Owner: Finance. Read together with the expense policy. Where the two differ, this
document wins for travel-related spend.

## Booking

All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the company travel tool, **Waypoint**.
Bookings made outside Waypoint are reimbursed only if Waypoint was unavailable and the
employee says so on the claim.

Travel must be approved by the line manager before booking. Trips over **EUR 2,000**
total also need department head approval.

## Class of travel

- Trains: standard class for journeys under 3 hours, first class allowed for longer
  journeys.
- Flights: economy for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights
  of 6 hours or more. Business class requires Chief Financial Officer approval.
- Rail is preferred over air where the rail journey is under 5 hours door to door.

## Hotels

Hotel cost limits per night, including taxes:

| City type | Limit |
|---|---|
| Halcyon site cities (Rotterdam, Duisburg, Lyon, Gdansk) | EUR 140 |
| Other European cities | EUR 180 |
| Outside Europe | EUR 220 |

A hotel receipt is always required, regardless of amount. This overrides the EUR 25
receipt threshold in the expense policy.

## Per diems

Instead of claiming individual meals, travellers receive a per diem of **EUR 45 per full
day** away and **EUR 22 for a partial day** (departure or return day). Breakfast included
in the hotel rate reduces the per diem by EUR 10. Per diems replace meal claims; a
traveller cannot claim both for the same day.

## Ground transport

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transport is impractical, for example
before 06:00 or after 22:00, or when carrying equipment. Rental cars need manager
approval and must be the compact class unless three or more people travel together.

## Insurance and safety

All employees are covered by Halcyon's business travel insurance. Travellers to a country
with a government travel warning need Chief Operating Officer approval and must register
the trip with the security team at least **5 working days** before departure.
