# Time off policy

Owner: People team. Applies to all employees. Contractors follow their contract.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives **26 days** of annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays at their site. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated
amount.

- Leave is requested in the Atlas leave module and approved by the line manager.
- Requests for more than **10 consecutive working days** also need approval from the
  department head.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried into the next year. They must be used by
  **31 March** or they expire.
- Leave cannot be paid out except when employment ends.

## Sick leave

Employees who are unwell notify their manager before the start of their working day.
A medical certificate is required from the **fourth consecutive day** of absence.
Sick leave is paid in full for up to 6 weeks per year; beyond that, the site's statutory
scheme applies.

## Parental leave

Primary carers receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave. Secondary carers
receive **4 weeks** fully paid. Both may be taken any time within the first 12 months
after birth or adoption. Requests should be made at least 8 weeks in advance where
possible.

## Compassionate leave

Up to **5 paid days** are available on the death of an immediate family member. The
line manager can approve additional unpaid days.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the employee's contracted site, not their current location.
Remote employees follow the site of their contract.

## Volunteering

Every employee may take **2 paid days** per year for volunteering with a registered
charity. No manager approval is needed, but the days must be logged in Atlas.
